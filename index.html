Signal Summary

Eight years turning RF theory into networks that hold up under load.

TelecomNextGen™ is a telecom services practice built on 8 years of hands-on RF engineering — turning radio theory into networks that perform in the field, from macro site design and indoor DAS/IBS planning to KPI-driven optimization across 2G, 3G, LTE, and 5G.

We work fluently across Ericsson and Nokia platforms, using Atoll, NetAct, Actix, IWM and ENM to plan coverage, chase down interference, and keep capacity ahead of demand — for individual clients, enterprises, and operators alike.

Founder M.J.Z. led a cross-functional team through AT&T's SWAP & Modernization program, and previously delivered RF design and optimization for Rogers, T-Mobile, Verizon, and Sprint — experience that now underpins every TelecomNextGen™ engagement and training program.
